MedNova processes account details, appointments, doctor-issued prescriptions, uploaded reports and payment records to deliver hospital services. Patients can access their own records. Assigned doctors can access reports linked to their consultations. Reception staff can manage appointments and payment records. Pharmacists can access the prescription details needed to dispense medicines.

Video calls and service providers

MedNova does not record video calls. Temporary technical messages establish calls, and a configured relay may carry encrypted media. Email providers deliver password reset links. When online payments are enabled, the payment provider handles payment details; MedNova stores payment references and status, not card numbers.

Account security and access

Essential cookies maintain login sessions and protect forms. Passwords are hashed. Role checks restrict access through the application. Hospital infrastructure administrators can access database and backup systems as part of authorized operations. MedNova does not use advertising trackers.
